# Campos Personalizados — en-US

Descrição curta:

Sell personalized products with names, messages, images and buyer files. Get the details organized by order item. Start free.

Descrição completa:

Personalized orders start with the right details

Which name should you print? Which design did the customer choose? Where is their artwork? Collect these details on the product page with the purchase and reduce follow-up messages.

Campos Personalizados saves the answers with the order item in Tiendanube, so you can prepare each product as requested. Keep personalization details out of scattered conversations.

Made for gifts, stationery, shirts, party favors and made-to-order products.

Features for your store

- Short text, long text and numbers for names, messages and instructions.
- Selection lists and store images for covers, prints and designs.
- Buyer files (Beta): receive photos, logos and artwork in JPG, PNG or PDF. Pro: 1 file per field, up to 10 MB, with 2 GB storage. Ultra: up to 5 files per field, up to 20 MB each, with 10 GB. Download files in the app for 90 days after the order.
- Required fields and character limits to guide customers.
- Fields configured per product, with answers linked to the item in the cart, checkout and order.
- Recent personalized orders in the app.
- CSV export of personalized orders found among the store’s last 50 orders.

- Essential: USD 4.99/month.
- Pro: USD 7.49/month.
