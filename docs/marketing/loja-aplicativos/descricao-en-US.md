Personalized orders start with the right details

Collect names, choices and artwork on the product page. Campos Personalizados saves the answers with the order item in Tiendanube: clearer production instructions and fewer follow-up messages.

Made for gifts, stationery, shirts, party favors, kits and made-to-order products.

Features for your store

- Short text, long text and numbers for names, messages and instructions.
- Lists, colors and multiple choices to combine preferences, with selection limits.
- Store images to present prints and designs on paid plans.
- Options with quantities on Pro and Ultra to build bouquets, kits and baskets, with total unit limits. No price or inventory per option.
- Buyer files (Beta) on Pro and Ultra: photos, logos and artwork in JPG, PNG or PDF, with downloads in the app.
- Required fields, character limits and reusable templates.
- Details per item in the cart, checkout and order.
- View and export recent personalized orders as CSV.

Monthly plans in US dollars (USD)

- Free: start free and collect names, messages and choices with the order.
- Essential: USD 4.99/month. Colors, multiple choices and store images. E.g., shirt color and a name to print.
- Pro: USD 7.49/month. Everything in Essential + quantities and buyer files (Beta). E.g., a customer-built kit with their artwork.
- Ultra: USD 14.99/month. Everything in Pro + multiple files per field, more storage and priority support. E.g., photos for a collage.
