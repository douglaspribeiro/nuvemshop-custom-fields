## Prompts usados

### Banner

Use case: ads-marketing. Brand: Campos Personalizados, a SaaS app for Tiendanube that collects customer personalization details on product pages; it does not manufacture physical products or automatically preview artwork. Match existing brand palette: warm off-white #fbfcf9, forest green #14745c, dark green #18312c, soft sage. Elegant restrained premium design, professional editorial product photography with a simple software form illustration. No Creem/Stripe logos, no prices, no fabricated awards, no payment security claims, no upload functionality, no garbled filler text, no watermark. Asset type: horizontal checkout brand banner, landscape 3:1 composition, ideally 1536x512. Left half: exceptionally clear large title 'Campos Personalizados' and smaller Spanish line 'Cada pedido, a su medida.' with generous whitespace. Right half: a tasteful white ceramic mug and a sage notebook on a cream studio surface, each with understated small personalized name 'Sofía', accompanied by a floating white form card showing exactly 'Nombre' and value 'Sofía', and 'Mensaje' with value 'Para ti'. Show the form as conceptual detail collection, not live artwork editing. Subtle soft shadows, refined green accents, all essential content comfortably inside central 80% for cropping. Premium polished simplicity, readable typography.

### Produto

Use case: ads-marketing. Brand: Campos Personalizados, a SaaS app for Tiendanube that collects customer personalization details on product pages; it does not manufacture physical products or automatically preview artwork. Match existing brand palette: warm off-white #fbfcf9, forest green #14745c, dark green #18312c, soft sage. Elegant restrained premium design, professional editorial product photography with a simple software form illustration. No Creem/Stripe logos, no prices, no fabricated awards, no payment security claims, no upload functionality, no garbled filler text, no watermark. Asset type: square checkout product image, 1024x1024. A reusable image for all subscription tiers, not a plan-specific ad. Center composition: an elegant sage notebook with small name 'Sofía' and a white ceramic mug with same name, accompanied by one crisp white form card with two input rows labeled exactly 'Nombre' and 'Mensaje'. Background warm off-white with a very subtle sage shape, soft studio light and premium tactile materials, roomy margin of at least 12% around all content, clear silhouette and strong readability as a small thumbnail. Communicate digital personalization software through the form card, not a physical product shop.
